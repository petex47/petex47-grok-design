The first idea

The idea for Kaizen Fuel Kitchen started small — a name, a logo scribbled on paper, and a few smoked-chicken meals cooked out of a home kitchen for friends who trained hard and needed real food.

Along the way

Becoming a builder

Somewhere between shifts I started reading — $100M Offers, Show Your Work, anything I could get my hands on. Long mornings at coffee shops turned into the real school. Cooking taught me to execute. Books taught me to build.

Now

The work continues

These days the routine is written down: train, fuel, build. Backpack packed the night before, run group in the morning, kitchen and code the rest of the day. The work isn't glamorous, but it's mine — and it keeps compounding.

2013 – 2025

The line cook years

Twelve years on the line. Long shifts, hot kitchens, prep lists, and dinner rushes. It taught me urgency, preparation, ownership, repetition, and how to perform when things got uncomfortable. I'm still using every one of those lessons.
